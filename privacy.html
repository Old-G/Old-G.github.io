Privacy Policy

OLD-G Media · effective 19 October 2026

OLD-G Media is a media project about artificial intelligence run by Gleb Zavalov. This policy covers this website and the apps OLD-G Media uses to publish its own posts: the OLD-G Media app on Meta (Instagram and Threads), the OLD-G Media app on Google Cloud (YouTube) and the OLD-G Media app on LinkedIn.

This website

The site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising, and has no forms. The hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability; OLD-G Media does not use them to identify visitors.

The publishing apps

The apps are used only by the owner of OLD-G Media to publish posts and videos to the owner's own accounts and to read those posts' statistics. They are not offered to other people and do not access anyone else's account.

  • Through OAuth the owner grants the apps access to his own accounts: uploading videos to the OLD-G News YouTube channel; publishing posts and reading insights on his Instagram and Threads accounts; publishing posts on his LinkedIn profile.
  • The access tokens are stored on a private server operated by OLD-G Media, are used only to publish and to read statistics, and are never sold or shared with third parties.
  • The apps do not collect personal data of viewers, followers or commenters.

Google user data

OLD-G Media's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data received through the YouTube Data API is used only to upload videos to the owner's channel and is not used for advertising or transferred to others. YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google, Meta or LinkedIn account settings. On request to the address below, OLD-G Media deletes the stored tokens within 30 days.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: the.oldg.media@gmail.com.
